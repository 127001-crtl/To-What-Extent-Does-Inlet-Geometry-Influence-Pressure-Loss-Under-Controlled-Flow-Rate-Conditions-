A turbofan-style jet engine model, built in Onshape by following [this YouTube tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DE8bgbyb9xw).

**What I made:** a fan and nose cone at the front, a nacelle (outer duct) around a smaller core, compressor and turbine stages, fuel pipes, shafts, bearings, cases and brackets, and an exhaust section. A section (cutaway) view shows the inside.

This is a display model, not a working engine design.
